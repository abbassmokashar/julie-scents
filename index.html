Our craft

Small-batch perfumery, without the markup

Julie Scents began as a single counter in a fragrance hall, refilling bottles for clients who had fallen in love with a scent they could no longer afford to keep buying at retail.

Ten years on, the method has not changed. We buy premium oils in small quantities, keep our concentrations high, and refuse to cut a blend to make it cheaper. That is why a Julie Scents recreation can outlast the original it was built from.

High concentration

Blended at 25–30% oil, well above the industry norm for an eau de parfum.

Clean ingredients

Vegan, cruelty-free oils with no drying alcohol burn on first spray.
