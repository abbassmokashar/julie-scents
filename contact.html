Shipping & returns

The practical details

Orders placed before 3pm on a working day are blended and dispatched the same day. Anything containing a recreation takes one extra working day, because the blend has to settle before it travels.

Delivery options

  • Standard — three to five business days, free on orders over $150, otherwise $12.
  • Express — one to two business days, $22.
  • Boutique pickup — ready in four hours at Atelier 14, free.

Packaging

  • Every bottle travels in a rigid, unbranded outer box for discretion.
  • Two complimentary 5 ml sample vials are included with each order.
  • Gift wrapping and a handwritten note can be added at checkout for $6.

Returns

  • Unopened bottles may be returned within thirty days at our cost.
  • Recreations that fail the eight-hour test are replaced free of charge.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days.
