The atelier

Julie Scents began as a single refill counter in a fragrance hall. Ten years later we still work the same way — in small batches, at full strength, for people who love a scent enough to wear it every day.

Our story

It started with a bottle nobody could afford twice

Our founder spent years behind a fragrance counter, watching the same thing happen: a client would fall in love with a scent, buy a bottle, and then never replace it when it ran out.

The price was the problem, not the perfume. So she began blending small amounts herself — matching the accords from premium oils and refilling empty bottles for clients who could not justify another $150. Word travelled faster than any advertising campaign could have.

Today the atelier carries sixteen fragrances from thirteen houses, and every one of them can be recreated to order. The counter became a workshop. The approach never changed.

“Nobody should have to ration the thing that makes them feel like themselves.”

What we hold to

Three rules we do not bend

Authentic or nothing

Designer bottles come from authorised distributors only. If we cannot verify a source, it does not get sold.

Never dilute a blend

If a recreation cannot be made well, we would rather tell you no than sell you something thin.

Stand behind the hours

We publish a wear time, and we replace a bottle that does not meet it. That is the whole guarantee.
