Why ours lasts longer

Concentration is the whole story

A fragrance fades for one reason: there is not enough oil in it. Designer houses balance their formulas around cost, and the alcohol-to-oil ratio is where they economise.

We do the opposite. Our recreations are blended at 25–30% oil, which is why a Julie Scents bottle can still be readable on the skin long after the original has gone quiet. It is not magic — it is simply more perfume.
